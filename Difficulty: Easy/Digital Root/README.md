# Digital Root

## Problem Description

You are given a number `n`. You need to find the digital root of `n`. The digital root of a number is the recursive sum of its digits until we get a single digit number.

---

## Examples

### Example 1
- **Input:** `n = 1`
- **Output:** `1`
- **Explanation:** Digital root of 1 is 1.

### Example 2
- **Input:** `n = 99999`
- **Output:** `9`
- **Explanation:** Sum of digits of 99999 is 45 (not a single digit), and the sum of digits of 45 is 9 (a single digit).

---

## Constraints

- $1 \le n \le 10^7$
