# Primorial of a Number

Given a positive integer `n`, compute its **primorial**.

The primorial of `n` is the product of all prime numbers less than or equal to `n`.

## Examples

### Example 1
```text
Input: n = 5
Output: 30
```

**Explanation:**  
The prime numbers less than or equal to `5` are `2, 3, 5`.

```text
2 × 3 × 5 = 30
```

### Example 2
```text
Input: n = 12
Output: 2310
```

**Explanation:**  
The prime numbers less than or equal to `12` are `2, 3, 5, 7, 11`.

```text
2 × 3 × 5 × 7 × 11 = 2310
```

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ n ≤ 23`
